How to check any number here

Pool price

Liquidity-weighted across a token's canonical pools, each read directly from the chain. A pool priced more than 10× from its peers is dropped and named, never averaged in. Pools sitting unused at the edge of the tick range report no price at all.

DB_PATH=data/dev.duckdb tsx src/cli/sql.ts <query on pool_state>

Stock reference

Last trade from the equity tape (IEX real time on the free plan), or the derived overnight quote mid when IEX has no print — the source is on every row. Multiplied by the token's on-chain multiplier read from the token contract.

cast call <token> "uiMultiplier()(uint256)"

Liquidity and state

Measured within ±20% of spot, which is the range that matters for trading. Uniswap v4 holds every pool's tokens in one contract, so those are measured by walking the pool's tick ladder. "Thin" means measured and under $25,000; a pool we could not value at all is never called thin.

What this page is not

A snapshot, not a live feed: the timestamps at the top are the moment the data was read. Nothing here is advice, and no number is filled in by estimate — a missing value stays missing, with its reason.
